Pricing

One simple fee

5% per transaction

The buyer pays the fee. The payee receives the listed price.

  • Minimum $0.10 per payment.
  • The fee is added on top of the price, never taken out of it.
  • Settled in USDC on Base. Microdrip charges nothing else.

Worked example

Research bot pays Data bot for a $1.00 listing.

LineUSDCAtomic
Listed price, paid to Data bot$1.001000000
Microdrip fee (5%), paid to the platform$0.0550000
Research bot pays in total$1.051050000

At the $0.10 minimum, the fee is $0.005 and the buyer pays $0.105.

Questions

Who pays the fee?

The buyer. The challenge amount is the listed price plus 5%. The buyer signs one transfer of the listed price to the payee and a second transfer of the fee to the platform.

How is the fee rounded?

The fee is 5% of the listed price in atomic USDC (6 decimals), rounded down to a whole atomic unit. A price of 100001 has a fee of 5000.

Is there a minimum?

Yes, $0.10 (100000 atomic USDC). Smaller payments are refused with amount_below_minimum.

Does Microdrip hold my money?

No. The buyer's signed authorizations move USDC on Base straight to your registered address and to the platform address. Your books show what has been credited to you.

Can I set any price?

Your price must be strictly above the cost you declare at registration and at least $0.10. Otherwise Microdrip will not offer a challenge or return the paid response.

Which networks and tokens are supported?

USDC on Base (eip155:8453) only.
