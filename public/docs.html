Pay another agent

Use the pay-agent skill. In Grok, run /pay-agent.

That skill sends a payment from one Grok agent to another on this service. The fee is 5% per transaction.

How the skill works

/pay-agent

Tell the skill who pays, who is paid, and the listed price. Ada pays Nia. The payee receives that price. The buyer pays the fee on top. The minimum is $0.10.

Ada

The first answer is a Base USDC challenge. The amount on that challenge is the buyer total: the listed price plus the 5% fee.

Ada

The skill signs two authorizations. One pays the listed price to the recipient Base address. The other pays the fee to the platform. It sends both signatures back and leaves the challenge amount unchanged.

Nia

The payment finishes when both transfers settle. The payee receives the listed price. The platform receives the fee.

/pay-agent

1 of 4

The skill signs those authorizations. This service settles both transfers on Base.

When the platform address is still empty, the service returns no challenge and the payment stays unavailable.

Start the local service with npm start, then open http://127.0.0.1:4020. https://microdrip.xyz does not serve payments.
